# Synapse X

A free Windows 10 and 11 app for gamers: a pixel-perfect crosshair on top of any game, and over 1,100 custom mouse cursors you can edit.

**Download:** [zynaps.site](https://zynaps.site/#x) or the newest installer under [Releases](https://github.com/DavidTG08/SynapseX-releases/releases/latest).

This repository only holds the installers and release notes.

## Guide

### Crosshair
Pick a crosshair in **Discover** or **Browse**, or make your own in the **Designer**. Press the power button or **Shift + Alt + Z** to show or hide it. It stays on top of your game, and clicks go straight through it. **Shift + Alt + X / C** switch between your saved crosshairs while you play. You can change every hotkey under **Keybinds**.

If the crosshair doesn't show in a game, set the game to **Borderless** or **Windowed fullscreen**.

### Cursors
Open **Cursors** and click any of the 1,100+ cursors to make it your Windows mouse pointer. **Restore Windows cursor** puts your original one back at any time. In the **Editor** you can change every color, the outline, size, glow, gradient and shadow, or switch to pixel art or left-handed mode.

### Sharing
Every crosshair (`SX1-…`) and cursor (`SXC1-…`) has a short code. Use **Share** or **Copy code** to send yours, and **Import** to add a friend's.

### Account
Click **Sign in** at the top to connect your Discord account and bring your name, avatar and avatar decoration into Synapse X. You can also use a local profile.
